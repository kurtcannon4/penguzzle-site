Penguzzle

A daily balance puzzle. Every row is an ice floe resting on a rock, and where a penguin sits matters as much as which penguin it is.

Get it on the App Store

What it is

A penguin's weight counts for more the further out on the floe it sits, so you work out where everyone goes from the column totals and the igloo rules. It is the satisfaction of a nonogram, from arithmetic you can see happening.

One Daily for everyone

The same puzzle for every player, every day, with a result card you can share without spoiling it.

Always solvable

Every board is machine-checked to have exactly one answer before it ships. No guessing, ever.

No adverts, no accounts

Nothing is collected about you, and the whole game works offline.

Free to start

The Daily and its streak are free forever. One optional purchase opens the rest — it is not a subscription.
